Eclectic Golf Scorecard – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short:
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone.
  • No account, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not send your data to us or to anyone else.
  • The camera is only used to read card-swapping QR codes, and nothing is recorded.

Who we are

Eclectic Golf Scorecard (“the app”) is published by Off Script Logic. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at service-help-mail@protonmail.com.

Information the app keeps

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • your gross scores for each hole and round, and the dates rounds were played;
  • the name you type in (shown to a playing partner when you swap cards);
  • your settings – number of rounds, extra rounds, theme and sound;
  • a friend’s card while you are marking it, until you clear it.

We do not collect, receive, store or sell any of this information. It never leaves your device unless you choose to share it (see below).

Camera

The app asks to use the camera only when you tap Scan their code or Scan my scores on the Swap tab, so it can read a QR code shown on another phone. Images are processed on your device in real time and are not saved or sent anywhere. You can refuse or withdraw camera access at any time in your phone’s settings; everything else in the app keeps working.

Card swapping

When you swap cards with a playing partner, the QR code contains only your name, the round number and the scores for each hole. It passes directly between the two phones by the camera – no internet, Bluetooth or server is involved.

Backups

If you tap Save a backup, the app creates a file containing your rounds, name and settings and opens your phone’s share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send or save it (for example your email, Google Drive or iCloud), and is then covered by that service’s own privacy policy.

Phone backups

Your phone’s own backup system (iCloud Backup or Google’s Android backup) may include the app’s data if you have that turned on. This is controlled by you and by Apple or Google, not by us.

Ratings and app stores

The app may ask once if you would like to rate it. On iPhone this uses Apple’s own rating window; on Android it can open the Play Store page if you choose to. Downloading the app and leaving a rating are handled by Apple’s App Store and Google Play under their own privacy policies.

Tips

Settings has an optional tip jar. If you choose to leave a tip, the payment is handled entirely by Apple’s App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. The app never sees your card or payment details, and a tip does not link any information about you to the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because no information leaves the device.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual rounds in History, start a new competition in Settings, or delete everything by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, mention it in the app’s update notes.
